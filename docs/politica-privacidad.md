### Qué datos maneja la app y dónde se guardan
| Dato | Para qué | Dónde se guarda |
|---|---|---|
| Fotos de tus prendas y datos de cada una (tipo, color, estación, uso, marca, talla, favorita, limpia/sucia) | Montar tu armario digital | Solo en tu móvil |

### Permisos
**Cámara.** Se usa solo cuando eliges hacer una foto a una prenda. La foto se procesa en tu móvil (incluido quitar el fondo) y se guarda en la app.

**Galería / fotos.** Se usa solo cuando eliges una foto existente para añadirla como prenda. La app solo accede a las fotos que seleccionas.

**Ubicación (opcional).** Se usa únicamente para saber el tiempo de tu zona y sugerirte outfits. Si no das el permiso, puedes **elegir una ciudad a mano** y todo funciona igual. Puedes retirar el permiso cuando quieras desde los ajustes del móvil.

### Consulta del tiempo (servicio externo)
Para mostrar el tiempo, la app envía a un servicio meteorológico externo (Open-Meteo, https://open-meteo.com/en/terms#privacy) las **coordenadas aproximadas** o la **ciudad** elegida, junto con la dirección IP inevitable en cualquier conexión a internet. No se envía ninguna foto, prenda, medida ni identificador tuyo. Ese servicio tiene su propia política de privacidad. Si no quieres esta consulta, no concedas la ubicación y no uses la sugerencia por tiempo.

### Copias de seguridad: exportar e importar
Puedes exportar tu armario a un archivo y volver a importarlo, por ejemplo al cambiar de móvil. **Tú decides dónde guardas ese archivo** (tu móvil, tu nube personal, un correo…). FitArchive no lo envía a ningún sitio ni lo guarda. Ten en cuenta que el archivo contiene tus fotos y medidas: guárdalo en un lugar de confianza. Al importar, solo se lee el archivo que eliges.

### Compartir un outfit
Al compartir un outfit como imagen (WhatsApp, stories…) eliges tú la app de destino. La imagen se entrega a esa app y desde ese momento se rige por sus condiciones.

### Conservación y borrado
Los datos permanecen en tu móvil hasta que los borres. Puedes eliminar prendas desde la app, o borrar todo desinstalando la aplicación o borrando sus datos desde los ajustes del móvil. Como no guardamos nada en servidores, no podemos recuperarlos: haz una copia exportada si quieres conservarlos.

- **No ads, no monetization, no analytics**: we use no tools that track what you do in the app.

### What data the app handles and where it is stored
| Data | Purpose | Where it is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Photos of your garments and details for each one (type, colour, season, use, brand, size, favourite, clean/dirty) | Build your digital wardrobe | On your phone only |
| Outfits, outfit calendar and travel packing lists | Organize and plan | On your phone only |
| Body measurements (height, bust, waist, hips, etc.) | Work out how each size fits you | On your phone only |
| Likes and saved outfits | Learn your style for suggestions; computed on your phone | On your phone only |
| Approximate location or chosen city | Look up the weather | See "Location" below |

We do not collect data on any server of our own. We have no access to your wardrobe, photos or measurements.

### Permissions
**Camera.** Used only when you choose to take a photo of a garment. The photo is processed on your phone (including background removal) and stored in the app.

**Photo library.** Used only when you choose an existing photo to add as a garment. The app only accesses the photos you select.

**Location (optional).** Used only to find the weather where you are and suggest outfits. If you don't grant it, you can **pick a city manually** and everything works the same. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone settings.

### Weather lookup (external service)
To show the weather, the app sends your **approximate coordinates** or the **city** you chose to an external weather service (Open-Meteo, https://open-meteo.com/en/terms#privacy), along with the IP address that any internet connection inevitably involves. No photos, garments, measurements or personal identifiers are sent. That service has its own privacy policy. If you don't want this lookup, don't grant location and don't use weather-based suggestions.

### Backups: export and import
You can export your wardrobe to a file and import it again, for example when changing phones. **You decide where to keep that file** (your phone, your personal cloud, email…). FitArchive does not send or store it anywhere. Note that the file contains your photos and measurements, so keep it somewhere you trust. When importing, only the file you pick is read.

### Sharing an outfit
When you share an outfit as an image (WhatsApp, stories…), you choose the destination app. The image is handed to that app and is governed by its terms from then on.

### Retention and deletion
Data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can remove garments in the app, or erase everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your phone settings. Since nothing is stored on servers, we cannot recover it: export a backup if you want to keep it.

### Children
FitArchive is not directed at children under [PENDIENTE: minimum age, e.g. 14 in Spain] and we do not collect data from anyone.

### Your rights
Since we don't hold your data, there is nothing to request from us: you control it on your phone. If you believe we process any data about you, write to [PENDIENTE: contact email]. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

### Changes to this policy
If anything changes (for example, if a feature requiring a connection is added someday), we will update this page and the date above, and notify you in the app before the change applies.
