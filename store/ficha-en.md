## Long description (both stores)
FitArchive turns your wardrobe into a digital closet and helps you put together outfits with the clothes you already have.

It's free, there's no account to create, and everything stays on your phone.

YOUR CLOSET, DIGITIZED
• Take a photo of each garment: the background is removed automatically.
• Organize by type (tops, trousers, dresses, shoes, accessories…), color and season.
• Sort by occasion: work, going out, casual…
• Combine filters to find exactly what you want.
• Like your favorites.
• Track what's clean and what's dirty, or set how many wears before a garment goes to the laundry and it becomes dirty by itself.

OUTFITS
• Build looks by hand, dragging garments onto a 2D avatar.
• Get suggestions based on the weather, the occasion (work, date, party, casual, sport), colors that go together and your style.
• Suggestions only use clean clothes.
• Share your outfit as an image on WhatsApp or in stories.

SIZES
• Save your measurements once.
• Enter brand, type and size for each garment and see whether it will fit snug, right or loose, along with your ideal size.
• Size charts for Inditex (Zara, Pull&Bear, Bershka, Stradivarius, Massimo Dutti), Mango, H&M, Brownie, Scalpers, Hollister and Brandy Melville; a generic European chart for other brands.

ORGANIZATION
• Look calendar: what you wore each day.
• Travel suitcase with outfits and a clothing list.
• Weather from your phone's location, or a city you choose if you'd rather not grant permission.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• No sign-up, no profiles, no community.
• Everything lives on your phone.
• Export and import a copy of your closet as a file.

Designed with a fashion-magazine look: white, editorial type and burgundy.
